The three worlds

Every strategy runs on a cadence. Pick yours.

Fund LLC doesn't sort capital by asset class first — it sorts by how long you're willing to wait for it back. Each world is its own pool, its own clock, its own community of allocators and strategists.

Day / Day

Month / Month

Year / Year

The world for strategies that compound quietly, and allocators playing the long game.
